Privacy

LAST UPDATED 20 JULY 2026

The short version: I collect your email so I can send you the newsletter. I never sell it. There are no cookies and no tracking on this site. You can leave with one click, from any email I send.

AI with Pelumi is a newsletter run by Pelumi Fatoye in Lagos, Nigeria. This page explains what happens to your data when you subscribe, and what you can do about it.

What I collect

Only when you subscribe. Browsing the site collects nothing.

  • Your email address. The only thing required.
  • Your first name, if you choose to give it.
  • A consent record — the signup wording you agreed to, the date and time, and the IP address and browser signature of the request. This is how I can prove you actually subscribed, and how I keep bots out.

Subscribers who joined through the old Substack publication were carried over with their original signup date, and their consent record notes that origin.

No cookies, no tracking

This site sets no cookies, uses no local storage, and runs no analytics or advertising trackers. Nothing follows you between visits, and there is nothing here to opt out of.

One thing worth naming: the page loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers and Google receives your IP address as part of that request. That happens on page load, not at signup.

What it's used for

One thing: sending you the AI with Pelumi newsletter. No advertising profiles, no selling or renting your address, no sharing it with anyone else for their marketing.

Who processes it

Three services handle data on my behalf, each acting only on my instructions:

  • Supabase — hosts the subscriber database.
  • Resend — delivers the emails and keeps a mirror of the mailing list. Resend processes data in the United States.
  • Vercel — hosts this website and the signup endpoint. Standard server logs, including IP addresses, pass through it.

How long I keep it

Until you unsubscribe or ask me to delete you. When you unsubscribe, your address stays on an internal suppression list so I don't accidentally email you again. Ask and I'll erase it entirely.

Your choices and rights

  • Unsubscribe — one click, in the footer of every email, effective immediately.
  • Access, correct, or delete your data — email me and I'll handle it within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under the Nigeria Data Protection Act, the GDPR, or similar law. Ask and I'll honour them.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the newsletter will say so before the change takes effect.
