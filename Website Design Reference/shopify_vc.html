Enabling infrastructure and tooling for developers

" We started Graphite by rebuilding the fast, intuitive, collaborative tools we missed from places like Meta and Google. Since then, Graphite has grown into something bigger: a reimagined developer experience where speed and quality coexist. AI is changing how we write code, but code review hasn’t kept up. As code generation accelerates, code review has become both a bottleneck and a focus of software development. Graphite is rethinking code review from the ground up by giving developers the ability to get context quickly and stay in flow. Today, teams use Graphite every day to ship faster with confidence. "

Merrill Lutsky , Co-founder & CEO

Maximizing leverage for entrepreneurs

" We started Gumloop because we were seeing thousands of people who were unable to get anything working in the early days of agents. They knew exactly what they wanted to automate, but hit this wall where they either needed expensive engineers or had to give up. That's when it clicked. Gumloop’s core purpose is to democratize AI automation, allowing any person or entrepreneur, regardless of technical background, to have the impact of an AI engineer by easily building workflows. "

Max Brodeur-Urbas , Co-founder & CEO
